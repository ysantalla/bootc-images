# The XPS 16 (`xps16-da16260`)

What needs the real machine. `docs/xps16-checklist.md` has the checks to run on it.

## Verified

**Read on the XPS 16 (`45.20261125`, kernel 7.3.4-300.fc45, BIOS 1.13.0, board 0W09YJ, 2026-11-25), without root:**
- The machine runs the published image with a signed origin, with no layered packages shown. `katze-update.service` ran at 06:34 and found no new image. The rollback target is `base` from an unverified origin. `fstab` was written by Anaconda.
- `katze-setup.service` and `katze-flatpaks.service` finished, the user is in `incus-admin`, the default zone is `katze-laptop`, and the user has Katze Dark, its icons and Inter.
- No `katze-*` unit failed, and neither did `mcelog.service`. Failed: `systemd-remount-fs.service` (the overlay on `/` cannot be reconfigured), and the user's `grub-boot-success.service` (read-only file system), `plasma-startupsound.service` (sound not found) and `drkonqi-coredump-pickup.service` (time limit; there are no coredumps).
- thermald is active on this boot, and started in the same second. One boot only.
- `katze-xps-performance` reaches both platform profile handlers: `dell-pc` and `SoC Power Slider` read `performance`. The other three profiles were not tried.
- `vainfo` loads iHD 26.4.0, with HEVC and AV1 decode and encode.
- 3200x2000 at 120 Hz. KWin reports VRR, HDR and wide color gamut as incapable. The kernel applies its "disable Panel Replay" quirk.
- Charge limits read 50 and 80, with `charge_types` on `Custom`. At 70% on AC the battery does not charge.
- One suspend to s2idle, about 24 s, with no failure. After it, the kernel logged `intel-ipu7: Failed to get runtime PM`.
- `cam -l` lists the front camera (`ov08x40`), and the kernel finds `HIMX1092`.
- Internal camera, 2026-11-25, kernel 7.3.4 and libcamera 0.7.2. Requests of 1920x1080 or less use the sensor's 1928x1088 mode: the kernel logs `error group 3 code 18` and libcamera aborts. Inside WirePlumber that restarts the sound, which happened three times during the tests, so any application that takes the default camera does it. 2560x1440 and 3840x2160 use the full mode and work, upside down. With the GPU, which libcamera uses by default, they are scaled without cropping.
- `camera.py` from a checkout, not from the image: Chrome 158 and Brave Origin 158 with an empty profile and `WebRtcPipeWireCamera`, at 640x480, 1280x720 and 1920x1080 (Brave before the capture moved to its own process). The first frame took 0.8 to 1.1 s, then about 29 fps. Chrome's video process crashed with NV12 and works with YUY2. The picture is a bit green and noisy: libcamera has no tuning for this sensor. The owner used it in a call in Chrome, turning it on and off seven times, and found it fine (relay before the own process).
- In one process, libcamera left two threads and a dmabuf behind each time the camera was on (13 of each after 13). With the capture in its own process, nothing was left after 13, and it uses about 6% of one core in the relay and 12% in the capture, with 220 MB in total. Killing the capture brought the picture back in about 2 s, and stopping the service while in use left nothing running.
- `60-katze-camera.conf` in a private PipeWire and WirePlumber: only the relay's camera is listed. The image's raw nodes appear without its second half, since libcamera no longer claims them.
- ISH loads `ish_ptl` firmware. The NPU has the `intel_vpu` driver. `fprintd` finds no reader. `fwupdmgr get-updates`: none.

**`mise run power --load 60`, run by the owner (2026-11-25), on AC, `katze-xps-performance`, one run with `yes` on every CPU:**
- PL1 41 W, PL2 65 W, in `intel-rapl` and `intel-rapl-mmio`, the same as on battery. The task reads the limits before the load; they read the same again after it.
- Idle: 1.2 W of package power, 39 to 40 C.
- 0 to 26 s: 64.6 W on average, 92 to 100 C (100 C about 10 s in). 26 to 34 s: falls from 57 to 32 W. 34 to 58 s: steady at 31.6 W, 79 to 84 C, below PL1.
- What holds it at 31.6 W is unknown, since the limits were not read under load. The kernel counted 782 package throttle events (8.9 s) since boot.
- The owner is content with this behavior.

**Secure Boot on (`45.20261125`, kernel 7.3.4-300.fc45, 2026-11-25), read without root:**
- The owner turned it on in the BIOS. `mokutil --sb-state` says enabled, `bootctl` says deployed, and the kernel is in `integrity` lockdown. Two boots so far, with the published image and no key of our own.
- Failed units: `systemd-remount-fs.service` and the user's `grub-boot-success.service`, both of which also failed with it off. The kernel log has no module refused for its signature.
- thermald, tuned (`katze-xps-balanced`), Tailscale and firewalld are active, `dell-pc` and `SoC Power Slider` read `balanced`, `vainfo` loads iHD 26.4.0, and `/dev/kvm` and `/dev/tpmrm0` exist.
- The kernel restricts hibernation, which the owner's plan already rules out.

**One suspend with Secure Boot on (same image, 2026-11-25), started by the owner, on AC with the monitor connected, Balanced profile:**
- s2idle for about 16 minutes, one success and no failure. `suspend_stats/last_hw_sleep` reads 984 s of the 992 s between the start and the end of the script, which itself waits for about 6 s.
- Suspending devices took 0.57 s and resuming them 0.60 s. The kernel logged `intel-ipu7: Failed to get runtime PM`, as without Secure Boot, and the camera device was runtime suspended a few minutes later.
- After it: Wi-Fi and Tailscale connected, Bluetooth, PipeWire, WirePlumber, thermald and tuned active, both displays enabled, and the same two failed units as before. Nothing was played or looked at for this entry.
- The last wakeup event among devices was the Dell mouse receiver's, at resume. What woke the machine was not established.

**Voice dictation on the NPU (2026-11-25), on the XPS without root, in rootless containers without network:**
- Voxtype 1.3.1 (`onnx-avx2`), OpenVINO GenAI 2026.4.1 trimmed to NPU and CPU, Fedora's `intel-npu-driver` 1.32.0 and kernel 7.3.4 with its own firmware. Intel's driver package was not needed. `npu_busy_time_us` grew in each NPU run and not in the CPU ones.
- Six Spanish sentences read by people (FLEURS, 154 words): `large-v3-turbo-int8` got 2 words wrong and took 0.56 to 0.71 s for each on the NPU, 2.9 to 3.1 s on the CPU. `small-int8` got 9 wrong and took 0.30 to 0.50 s on the NPU, 0.65 to 0.86 s on the CPU. Clean recordings, not the XPS's microphones.
- On the GPU (Arc B390, `intel-opencl` 26.40, OpenVINO's GPU plugin), the same texts: turbo 0.32 to 0.46 s for each sentence and small about the same, 3 s to compile the first time and 0.3 s to load after it. Its memory was not measured (the process shows 0.4 GB, without what the GPU holds), nor the power of either.
- Asked for the NPU in a container without `/dev/accel`, Voxtype went on with the GPU. `dictation-setup.sh gpu` and back to `npu` worked in the built image.
- Size: the machine's own layer is 489 MB without the GPU and 1.01 GB with it, uncompressed, mostly LLVM and clang.
- First compilation for the NPU: 148 s (turbo), 29 s (small). After it, 0.86 s to load from the cache, which takes 2.3 GB in the home. Loading and transcribing peaked at 4 GB of memory, so the model is only loaded while dictating.
- The image built rootless on the published `base`: lint passes, `tests/smoke.sh` has 0 failures, and inside it `dictation-setup.sh` (with the model already there) and four transcriptions worked.

**Voice dictation in a VM (2026-11-25), this branch with the IR reader's and the documentation's, installed with `bootc install to-disk` from a throwaway Fedora VM and run with QEMU as the user, no root on the XPS:**
- The VM boots to the Katze desktop with no failed units. Without NPU or GPU, Voxtype goes on with the CPU.
- A recording played into a virtual microphone, Meta+H before and after it: the sentence arrived with its accents in Konsole (`cat > file`) and in a Chrome text box, and the clipboard had what it had before. In KWrite the window had no text area open, so that says nothing.
- It found that KDE ignores a `kglobalaccel` file with `NoDisplay=true`. The fixed file was tried in the home directory of that VM, not from a rebuilt image.
- Voxtype logs `model 'large-v3-turbo-int8' is not downloaded` at start, because its catalog does not have that model. It works anyway.

**The infrared camera's reader, without root and without the sensor (2026-11-25):**
- libcamera 0.7.2, `cam -l` in bwrap: with any one of `/dev/video16`, `/dev/v4l-subdev2` or `/dev/video0` denied, it lists no camera. So the infrared nodes cannot be taken from the user without losing the normal camera.
- `hm1092` with the CAP_SYS_RAWIO check compiles for 7.3.4-300.fc45. Not signed or loaded.
- `ir-camera` passes its self-test, also with the address and undefined behavior sanitizers. The image was built rootless on the published `base`: lint passes, `tests/smoke.sh` has 0 failures, and gcc is not in it.
- The lock screen's PAM worker (kscreenlocker 6.8.3) is not setuid and runs as the user, read from its source and its file mode: whatever recognizes a face there cannot read a root-only camera by itself.

## Not tested yet

- `katze-ir-camera.socket` on the machine: that the reader works under its sandbox, the exposure in a dark room and in sunlight, the lamp going off on a kill, a client that leaves and a suspend, and that a program of the user's is refused and the normal camera still works after that.
- Dictation by hand: the model's download, the XPS's microphones, pasting in Brave, Zed, Slack and KWrite, the NPU after a suspend, and its effect on battery.
- With Secure Boot on: `mise run power`.
- The camera relay from the built image: `katze-camera.service` at login, a real video call, Firefox, Slack and other Flatpaks, suspend with the camera on (the relay turns it off, through a logind delay) and a suspend after using it.
- The screen turning off after 3 minutes on AC and 2 on battery on `xps16-da16260`. `tests/smoke.sh` reads the values back as a new user would; PowerDevil acting on them was not seen. The key names come from PowerDevil's libraries in the image.
- thermald over two or three cold boots, and the power saver, balanced and balanced on battery profiles.
- The limits while loaded, and `mise run power` in the balanced profile.
- Intel hardware decode in Haruna on the XPS. `vm-up` sound was heard in YouTube and in Haruna, in the VM.
- On the XPS: VRR and display glitches (`kscreen-doctor -o`), Intel encode and decode in Chrome (steps in `README.md`), Wayland screen sharing in a real call, temperature, battery and suspend.
- Whether the Intel video flags in `xps16-da16260` are needed, or do anything: compare a real video with and without them on the XPS. Chromium's own `vaapi.md` uses `AcceleratedVideoEncoder`; the `Vaapi*` names are said to be from before Chromium 131.
- A suspend on battery and its drain, one overnight, sound and the displays after resume.
